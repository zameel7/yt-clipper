// what's in the box

Runs entirely on your machine

No server, no upload, no account. Bundled binaries do every bit of the work.

🤖

AI clip suggestions

Gemini 2.5 Flash (free tier) reads the transcript and picks the moments worth sharing.

✂️

Real local cuts

yt-dlp downloads, ffmpeg cuts. Output is a normal .mp4 — no watermarks.

⏱️

Frame-level in/out

Nudge the start and end of every clip before you cut. You keep final say over the edit.

▶️

Inline preview

Watch the exact clip range in an embedded player before committing to the cut.

🖼️

Frames & overlays

Color borders or upload your own PNG overlay to brand every clip you export.

🔑

Your key, stored locally

Bring your own free Gemini key. It's saved on your device and never sent anywhere else.
